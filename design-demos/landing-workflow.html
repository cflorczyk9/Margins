A product tour, not a pitch

Drop files in. Get a wiki out. Files stay on your disk.

Margins is a vault-builder. Hand it transcripts, PDFs, notes, screenshots. It drafts source pages, concept pages, and entity pages — and waits for you to approve them before anything lands.

Bring your own Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic key. Margins makes the call. The result is plain markdown in a folder you control.

The loop
01
ingest

Drop a file or point at a folder.

Drag in a Granola transcript, a YouTube link, a PDF, a screenshot, a thread you pasted into a .txt. Margins reads from your disk.

Nothing leaves your machine yet. Your originals get filed into raw_sources/ — Margins never edits them.

02
draft

Margins drafts what it would file.

It reads the source through your AI key — Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic — and proposes pages: one source page, plus candidate concepts and entities mentioned inside.

You're paying the API. Margins makes the request and parses the response. No relay, no servers in the middle.

03
review

Review every page before it lands.

You see every proposed page side-by-side with what's already in your vault. Approve, edit, or reject — per file. Nothing is written until you say so.

Originals stay in raw_sources/ either way. Rejecting a draft costs you nothing.

04
grow

The vault grows.

Approved pages land in wiki/ — under sources/, concepts/, entities/, or synthesis/. Frontmatter, body, citations back to the source file. Plain markdown.

Each new page knows where it came from. Click a citation and you're back at the raw transcript.

05
own

Read your vault in anything.

It's a folder of markdown. Open it in Obsidian, VS Code, vim, Notes.app. Push it to git. Grep it. Back it up. Walk away from Margins entirely if you want to.

Frontmatter is YAML. Data files are JSON. No proprietary format, no lock-in, no export-button-as-favor.

06
operate

An operating layer ships with the vault.

Every new vault includes operator-manual.md, query-cookbook.md, and folders of commands/ and agents/. They tell any LLM how to read, write, and link inside this vault.

Point Claude Code, Cursor, or any agent at the folder. The agent reads the manual and behaves consistently. You didn't have to prompt-engineer it.

Three rules Margins runs on
rule 01

Two folders, one rule.

raw_sources/ holds the originals you brought in. wiki/ holds AI-generated pages. Margins writes only into wiki/ — the sources are never edited.

rule 02

Bring your own AI.

Plug in a Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic key. Margins makes the API call from your machine. No Margins servers, no telemetry, no relay.

rule 03

Reviewed-first ingest.

Every page Margins drafts is shown to you first — sources, concepts, entities. Approve, edit, or reject per file before anything touches the vault.

What Margins is for

Margins is the engine behind Briefly — a meeting-prep tool for wealth advisors — but the vault itself is general. If you take a lot of notes, sit in a lot of meetings, and want the things you read and heard to compound into something you can search and trust, this is the loop.

If you want to see the format up close, the repo has a sample vault built from real source files.
