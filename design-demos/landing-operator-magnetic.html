Two folders, one rule.

Your originals stay where you put them. Margins writes generated knowledge into a sibling folder, never on top of your evidence.

raw_sources/

Your originals. Margins never edits these.

  • meeting-2026-04-30.txt
  • karpathy-talk.md
  • pricing-deck.pdf

You drop. You write. Margins reads.

wiki/

AI-generated pages. Cited back to raw_sources/.

  • sources/
  • concepts/
  • entities/
  • synthesis/

Margins writes here. Every page cites a source.

Three rules Margins runs on.

Source-separated, by design.

Your evidence and the AI's writing live in different folders. The two never blur.

Bring your own AI.

Plug in a Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic key. No Margins servers, no key sync, no telemetry.

Reviewed-first ingest.

See every page Margins is about to write. Approve, edit, or reject per file before anything lands.

Instructions for AI come bundled in. Any AI that can read files knows how to work inside the folder.
