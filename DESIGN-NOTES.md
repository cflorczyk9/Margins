# Margins Design Notes

## Graph Redesign Required

The current graph tab is not accepted as the final direction. It needs a full design pass instead of incremental polish.

Target direction:
- Feel closer to Obsidian: spatial, calm, draggable, and clearly connected.
- Avoid clip-art circles and generic AI/dashboard styling.
- Keep edges visually behind nodes so lines never appear through dots.
- Make node interaction obvious: click, drag, hover, and open linked notes.
- Revisit the whole graph composition, not just colors and force settings.

## Layout Notes

- The Inbox drop zone should keep the artistic Kandinsky-style Margins visual language while staying simple.
- Theme switching belongs in the bottom-left sidebar and should be labeled clearly.

The drop zone now carries a Kandinsky composition (yellow disk with vermillion + ink core, cobalt arc, open black triangle, scattered violet/green/ink dots) behind the dashed frame at 0.5 opacity, waking to 0.85 on hover or drag. The "Endpoint inbox polish" stayed quiet on the *frame*; the *art* lives inside.
